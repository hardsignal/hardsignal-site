# Phase 1 — RF DoA Characterisation

KrakenSDR RF observation and DoA experiments using controlled 433 MHz TPMS transmitters.

## Result

Observed: **433.868160 MHz**

Dominant repeatable DoA clusters:

- **64.79° ± 1.06°** — N=81
- **123.31° ± 1.26°** — N=16
- **342.60° ± 3.60°** — N=20
- **228.82° ± 3.35°** — N=11

The measurements demonstrated repeatable DoA estimation and highlighted the effects of array geometry, reflections and multipath.

**Phase 1: Complete.**
